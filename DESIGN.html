Matcha Diary

Warm ceramic minimal. Paper-toned surfaces, one matcha accent, monospace reserved for data, near-flat corners. The photographs carry the colour; the interface stays out of their way.

This page is built from the tokens it documents — what you read is what you are looking at. Light-only: the system defines no dark palette.

Overview

brand & style

Matcha Diary is a diary, not a scoreboard. People log the bowl they drank, the photograph they took of it, and what they noticed — and the interface's job is to stay out of the way of those three things. Every surface is a warm paper tone so that photographs of green tea, which are almost always a narrow band of green and beige, look like the brightest object on the screen.

The personality is warm ceramic minimal: quiet, tactile, and a little utilitarian. It should feel like a well-kept notebook rather than a social product. When a rule or token does not cover a case, resolve it toward less: fewer weights, flatter corners, more whitespace, no new colour.

Green means state or data

The accent appears on the active nav item, the filled cells of a rating bar, and the single primary action on a screen. If green is being used because a surface looked plain, it is being used wrongly.

selected

There is no overall score

A cup is described by the shape of its individual taste ratings and by what the author wrote. Nothing is averaged, nothing collapses to a star count, and ratings never appear in the timeline — only on the review page. Do not add a summary number, however convenient it would be for sorting.

1 accent · 1 warm neutral family · no second hue

The palette is deliberately small so that the only saturated thing on a screen is either a photograph or a piece of interactive state.

Typography

Inter for voice · JetBrains Mono for fact

Two families, and the split between them is semantic rather than aesthetic. Inter carries anything a person wrote in their own voice. JetBrains Mono carries anything that is a fact about the cup — café, date, handle, taste notes, axis labels, rating values. Reading a screen, you can tell authored text from recorded data without reading a word of it.

Inter — the author's voice

display36 / 600 / 1.15 / -0.02em A diary for every bowl
headline-lg24 / 600 / 1.25 / -0.015em Uji Ceremonial, whisked thin

body-prose18 / 400 / 1.75 · review diary body The front is all cut grass and something like snap peas. Then it turns: a long savory tail that coats the roof of the mouth.
body-lg18 / 400 / 1.55 · lead-in Ordered it thin on purpose — the shop grinds a first-harvest Uji.
body-md15 / 400 / 1.6 · default Water was noticeably softer than what I get at home.
body-excerpt15 / 400 / 1.65 · feed, clamped to 4 lines Sifted the powder for once and it made an absurd difference — not one lump, not one gritty sip.
label-lg13 / 500 / 1.2 · button text Publish entry

JetBrains Mono — recorded fact

data-md13 / 400 / 0.02em / tabular KETTL TEA · BROOKLYN · 2026-08-04 · 4.5
data-sm11 / 400 / 0.04em · chips, hints umami · cut grass · astringency
label-caps11 / 400 / 0.12em / uppercase Taste notes
label-axis11 / 400 / 0.1em / uppercase · 84px column aftertaste
data-xs9 / 400 / 0.08em · on photos only COVER

Weight is rationed: 400 for prose, 500 for button labels and an author's display name, 600 for headlines. Nothing is bold, nothing is italic, and no screen shows more than these three weights. Uppercasing happens in CSS, never in the source text.

The mid-range steps — 12, 16, 24 — do most of the work.

Content widths

content-max 1080
reading-max 720
timeline-max 620
topbar-height 60

1080 for chrome and reference pages (24px gutter). 720 for the review and the composer — wide enough for a 3:2 cover, narrow enough that an 18px diary body stays near 70 characters. 620 for the feed, where the gutter drops to 16px so photographs get the width back on a phone.

Posts are separated by a single hairline rather than by gaps or shadows, and hover paints a sunk wash that bleeds 16px past the row so the whole post reads as one target. Breakpoints are content-driven, not device-driven: 640px collapses the two-column rating panel, 600px reflows paired form fields and drops the photo tray from four columns to three, 760px collapses the reference grids.

Elevation & Depth

hairlines and tone, not shadow

The stack is shallow on purpose — a diary page should feel like paper on a desk, not like cards floating over a void. Every shadow is warm-tinted rgba(48, 42, 30, …), never neutral black.

Level 0 · page Paper background. No border, no shadow.
Level 1 · resting Cards, inputs, ghost buttons. 1px hairline does the separating. 0 1px 2px / .05
Level 2 · photography The review cover — the one visible shadow in the system. 0 2px 10px / .07
Level 3 · reserved Overlays and menus. No current screen uses it. 0 12px 32px / .10

Sticky chrome uses translucency plus a 12px blur instead of a shadow, to signal that content is passing underneath it. Recession is expressed with surface-sunk — an empty rating cell reads as a groove waiting to be filled rather than as an outlined box.

Radius is assigned by material, not by size — the same button is 3px whether it is 28px or 40px tall.

The zero is the load-bearing decision. A taste note is a stamped label, not a bubble — square corners, monospace, no icon, no dot. It is the sharpest shape in the system and the only component that never softens, which is what makes a row of them read as a record rather than as tags on a blog post.

Components

buttons · chips · ratings · cards · inputs

Buttons

three levels · at most one primary per screen

The button-sm variant swaps the Inter label for mono at 11px, so small controls read as data rather than as shrunken buttons. All three levels sit at sm (3px) and depress 1px on :active.

Taste-note chips

rounded.none · mono 11 · overflow is cut, not scrolled
umami cut grass snap pea selected astringency

Resting: white fill, outline border. Selected: primary-container fill with accent border and text. In the composer, chips gain a × and the suggestion row moves a chip into the note box on click. The feed shows up to four.

Rating bars

0–5 in half steps · 5 cells · no overall score

Reading — 10px cells

umami 4.5
sweetness 3.0
bitterness 2.0
astringency 1.5

Rating — same component, 22px cells

aftertaste5.0
aroma3.0

The fourth and fifth cells above show the hover preview in primary-outline — a pending 4.5, lighter than a committed value.

The bar you read is the bar you click — there is no separate slider. Left half of a cell sets the half step, right half sets the whole, clicking the current value clears it to zero, arrow keys nudge by 0.5, Home and End jump to 0 and 5. The editable host exposes role="slider" with aria-valuenow and aria-valuetext. Axis names sit in a fixed 84px column, values in a 34px right-aligned column with tabular figures, so a stack of bars aligns on both edges.

The set of axes is not fixed. Six are suggested — umami, sweetness, bitterness, astringency, aroma, aftertaste — and the author drops, adds, or names their own. Astringency stays separate from bitterness on purpose: one is a taste, the other a mouthfeel.

Cards & photographs

selection is an outline, never a border
1 / 6

Review cover — 3:2 at lg (8px) with the level-2 shadow. The only 8px corner in the system.

Feed photograph — 4:3 at md (5px) with an outline-variant hairline.

Thumbnails and composer slots — 1:1 at sm (3px). Selection is a 2px matcha outline offset 2px, so the photograph never shifts.

Do's and Don'ts

guardrails

Do

  • ✓Keep green for state and data — active nav, filled rating cells, focus rings, the one primary button per screen.
  • ✓Put anything factual in JetBrains Mono and anything authored in Inter. The family is the signal.
  • ✓Show taste ratings on the review page only.
  • ✓Keep taste-note chips and rating cells at rounded.none. They are the system's fixed point.
  • ✓Express depth with a hairline and a tone change first; reach for a shadow only for photography and real overlays.
  • ✓Treat on-surface-muted as a large-text and non-text colour. At 3.5:1 it fails AA for body copy — darken toward #6F6A5E before shipping anything a user must read.
  • ✓Ship one rating control: the bar you read is the bar you click. Keep the keyboard path and the role="slider" semantics wherever it appears.
  • ✓Clamp the feed excerpt to four lines and cut chip overflow, so every post holds the same height rhythm.
  • ✓Define an error palette in DESIGN.md before building the first failure state.

Don't

  • ✕Use green as decoration: no green dividers, no green headings, no tinted section backgrounds because a page looked empty.
  • ✕Set a person's own words in monospace, or a date in the sans face.
  • ✕Introduce an overall score, an average, or a star count anywhere — not in the feed, not in sorting, not in a profile summary.
  • ✕Round anything past lg (8px), or add a circle outside the brand mark and avatars.
  • ✕Use neutral-black shadows — every shadow is warm-tinted rgba(48, 42, 30, …).
  • ✕Rely on placeholder (2.2:1) to communicate anything. It is a hint, and every field also needs a real label.
  • ✕Add a second rating input — no numeric field, no separate slider, no dropdown of values.
  • ✕Promote, feature, or visually rank a post. Every entry in the timeline is drawn identically.
  • ✕Derive colours from the .ph-* photo gradients; they are upload stand-ins, not tokens.
