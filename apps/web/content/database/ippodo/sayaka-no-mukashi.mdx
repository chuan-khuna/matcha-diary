# Sayaka-no-mukashi

The everyday one. Lighter body, brighter attack, less to think about — a powder to drink two bowls
of on a Tuesday rather than one bowl of on a Sunday.

Cheaper water and a careless 75° pour will not ruin it. The finish is short and clean, and a faint
pea-shoot sweetness shows up more on the second bowl than the first.
