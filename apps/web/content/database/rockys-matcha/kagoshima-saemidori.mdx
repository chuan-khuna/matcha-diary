# Kagoshima Saemidori

Kyushu rather than Kyoto. Saemidori runs bright and citrus-adjacent, and this is ground fine enough
that the astringency stays light even when the whisk is lazy.

The shortest finish on this list, and that is the point — it is clean, it resets the mouth, and it
does not coat anything on the way out.
