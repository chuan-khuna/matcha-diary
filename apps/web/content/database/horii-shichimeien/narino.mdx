# Narino

A single-cultivar Narino off the Okunoyama garden in Uji, a plot the house has worked since the Muromachi period. Horii's own copy puts its theanine at close to twice that of other matcha and describes a strong sweetness and umami over a full body.

Listed by Peace Oriental Teahouse (peaceteahouse.com), Bangkok. Sold out at the time of the listing.
