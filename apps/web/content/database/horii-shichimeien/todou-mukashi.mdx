# Todou Mukashi

A hand-picked blend of mostly Samidori, and the bitter end of the range: the listing is unusually blunt that the flavour is strong and almost entirely bitter. Worth knowing before it is whisked thin.

Listed by Peace Oriental Teahouse (peaceteahouse.com), Bangkok.
