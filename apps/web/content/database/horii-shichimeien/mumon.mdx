# Mumon

Asahi from the Okunoyama and Togawa gardens, and the tea the house puts into competition year on year. The listing describes it as well balanced rather than pointed in any one direction, and names no individual flavours — so this record carries none.

Listed by Peace Oriental Teahouse (peaceteahouse.com), Bangkok.
