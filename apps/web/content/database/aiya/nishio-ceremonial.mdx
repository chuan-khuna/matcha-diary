# Nishio Ceremonial

Nishio rather than Uji, and it tastes like it: softer, nuttier, less of the marine depth and more
of a toasted sweetness sitting where the umami would otherwise be.

Very forgiving. This is the one to hand someone who has only ever had matcha as a latte and wants
to try a bowl without deciding they hate it.
