# Kinrin

A koicha powder that behaves badly as usucha. Ground fine enough to go to paste with very little
water, and the Asahi in the blend brings a dark, nearly cocoa-like weight that Samidori on its own
never reaches.

Held against the tongue it thickens rather than thins. The sweetness arrives late — a good thirty
seconds after the swallow — and stays longer than anything else in this list.
