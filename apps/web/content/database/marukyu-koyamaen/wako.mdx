# Wako

The powder most people meet first, and the reason Samidori has the reputation it does. Sweet,
round, almost edgeless — it forgives water that is too hot and a whisk that is too slow.

Thin, it foams into a pale jade with a chestnut sweetness that sits at the front and never turns
bitter. Thick, it flattens out; this is not a koicha powder pretending otherwise.
