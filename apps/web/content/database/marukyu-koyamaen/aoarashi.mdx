# Aoarashi

The blend the cafés buy by the kilo. Loud, green, and built to survive dairy: pull it thin into oat
milk and it still reads as matcha rather than as a colour.

Bare, it shows its hand — a coarse grassiness and a bitterness that arrives early and then does not
develop. Less a flaw than a design brief.
