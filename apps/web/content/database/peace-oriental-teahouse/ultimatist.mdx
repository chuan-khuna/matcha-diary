# Ultimatist Matcha

Peace's own label, single flush — one picking rather than a blend across the year. Ultimatist is rung 3 of four: the ladder runs Idealist, Perfectionist, Ultimatist and Legendary, all at 20 g, and the price is the only thing the listing distinguishes them by.

No origin, cultivar or tasting notes are published for the own-label range, so this record carries none.

Listed by Peace Oriental Teahouse (peaceteahouse.com), Bangkok.
