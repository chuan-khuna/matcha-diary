# Asahi

The top of the card at 1,490 for 20 g — three hundred baht clear of either Hoshi and three times
Mito. Uji, ceremonial, and the only powder in the range Oneday describes as silky.

Sweet umami with a light marine note under it, finishing on vanilla and white chocolate. The
softest vocabulary on the card, and the most expensive.

No cultivar is published, so this record carries none — the name is the maker's, whatever the
cultivar of the same name would suggest.
