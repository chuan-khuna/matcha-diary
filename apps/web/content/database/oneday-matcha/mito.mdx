# Mito

The one powder on the card marked Premium rather than Ceremonial, and the cheapest thing Oneday
lists at 490 for 20 g — less than half of either Hoshi.

The listing says what it is for rather than describing it in the abstract: เหมาะกับลาเต้, suited to
a latte. Roasted rather than smoked, nutty, and rich enough to carry milk.

No cultivar is published, so this record carries none.
