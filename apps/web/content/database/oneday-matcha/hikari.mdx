# Hikari 光

The only Aichi powder on the card, and the one Oneday recommends by ease rather than by
character — ดื่มง่าย, easy to drink. Floral and aromatic, sweet at the finish, with an umami the
Thai line calls พอดี: enough, and no more.

Not the same tin as Uji Hikari, which is a different region at a different price. The kanji on this
one is 光.

No cultivar is published, so this record carries none.

- **Notes** — Floral, Highly Aroma, Sweetness, Gentle Umami
- **โน้ต** — ฟลอรัล หอม หวานปลาย นุ่ม อูมามิพอดี ดื่มง่าย
- **Grade** — Ceremonial · 20 g
