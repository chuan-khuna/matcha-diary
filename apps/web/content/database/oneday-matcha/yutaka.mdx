# Yutaka

The only Kyushu powder Oneday lists, from Shibushi on the Kagoshima coast, and the only one whose
note splits by preparation: clear and floral on its own with a slight bitterness at the finish,
turning creamy and cheese-like with milk.

Cheesy and avocado are the maker's words, and they are unusual enough to be worth keeping as
written — nothing else on the card reaches for either.

No cultivar is published, so this record carries none.

- **Notes** — Cheesy, Avocado, Floral, Buttery Rich
