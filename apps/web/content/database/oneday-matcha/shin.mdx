# Shin 真

The darkest reading in the Yame range and the cheapest of its ceremonial grades. Smoke again, but
the sweetness the two Hoshi lots finish on is replaced by dark chocolate, and the bean note is
specific: edamame rather than the general nuttiness the rest of the range lists.

No cultivar is published, so this record carries none.
