# Hoshi

Yame, and the card names Hoshinomura under it — the mountain village the leaf comes from, called
out separately from the Yame appellation the rest of the Fukuoka side carries. Hoshi and Hoshi02
are the only two marked that way, and they are the two most expensive powders Oneday lists.

Smoke is what the Yame range is built on: four of its five powders name it, and this one names it
without qualification. Nutty and creamy underneath, with a vanilla finish.

No cultivar is published, so this record carries none.

- **Notes** — Nutty, Smoky, Creamy Umami, Aroma, Vanilla
