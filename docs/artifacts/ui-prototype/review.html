Uji Ceremonial, whisked thin

KETTL TEA · BROOKLYN 2026-08-04 · 09:12 USUCHA

Ordered it thin on purpose — the shop grinds a first-harvest Uji that gets bullied by milk, so I wanted it bare. It arrives in a shallow bowl, foam still tightening.

The front is all cut grass and something like snap peas. Then it turns: a long savory tail that coats the roof of the mouth and refuses to leave for a good minute. Barely any bitterness, and what's there reads as structure rather than a flaw.

Foam held for a full three minutes before it broke. Water was noticeably softer than what I get at home, which is probably doing half the work here. Coming back with a thermometer.
