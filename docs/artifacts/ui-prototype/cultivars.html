Cultivar lineage

A powder is a blend somebody assembled and can change next season. A cultivar is a plant with a pedigree — and the pedigree is most of the answer to why two powders made the same way taste unalike. Every line below is a parentage; the way it is drawn is how well that parentage is attested.

This data is real. Unlike the powder records on the database page, the parentages, years, breeders and registration numbers here are sourced — see README.md. Only the taste notes are an editorial reading rather than a measurement.
